# FileReady privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

**Your files never leave your device.** (The optional research tools send only the search words you approve; see below.) FileReady reads, checks, fixes and builds PDFs and Word files inside the app on the phone or computer you are using. There is no account, no server and no upload.

## What is stored, and where
- The PDFs you add, the versions FileReady creates and the files it builds are saved in the app's private storage on your device.
- Your settings (name for reports, practice area, court, redaction list, and a salted one-way hash of your PIN) are saved on your device. The PIN itself is never stored.
- On Android, FileReady's data is excluded from phone backups, so client files are not copied to the cloud.
- Nothing is sent to Mark Joseph or anyone else. There are no analytics or ads.

## Text recognition
On the Android app the text-recognition engine is built in. In the web version, the first time you use text recognition your browser downloads the engine and English language files from a public code server (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Your pages are not sent: recognition runs on your device.

## Online research (optional)
The Patent check and Similar cases tools can search public databases. When you tap Search, only the search words shown on screen are sent: to the USPTO Open Data Portal (api.uspto.gov) or to CourtListener (courtlistener.com, run by Free Law Project). Your documents are not sent. When you open a result, FileReady downloads that opinion's text or that patent's claims document into the app so you can read it there. These services have their own privacy policies.

## Sharing
When you tap Share or Save, the file goes only where you send it (for example email or your Documents folder).

## Links
Rule packs link to official court and government websites. Opening a link takes you to that site, which has its own privacy policy.

## Deleting data
Settings > Delete all data on this device removes every filing, file and setting. Uninstalling the app also removes them.

## Security
See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how FileReady protects files and how to report a security problem.

## Not legal advice
FileReady flags common problems and prepares documents. It is not legal advice and does not guarantee a court or counterparty will accept a document.
